Privacy notice

Last change: 29 October 2026

Who is responsible

Chris Cook is responsible for this website. Email: info@tin.so

Your code stays in your browser

The editor makes the image in your browser. Your code does not go to a server. A share link keeps the code after the # of the address, and the browser does not send this part of the address to a server. Only the people who get the link from you can see the code.

Hosting by Cloudflare

Cloudflare, Inc. (101 Townsend St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA) hosts this website and delivers it to your browser. For each request, Cloudflare gets the data that your browser sends: your IP address, the date and time, the address of the page without the part after the #, the referrer, and the name and version of your browser. Cloudflare uses this data to deliver the website, to protect it from attacks, and to count the requests.

The legal basis is our legitimate interest in a safe and working website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Cloudflare processes the data for us under a data processing agreement. Cloudflare can process data in the USA. Cloudflare is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. More information is in the privacy policy of Cloudflare.

Statistics

We see the number of requests and visitors in the analytics of Cloudflare. These numbers come from the requests on the server. The website loads no analytics script, sets no cookies, and does not follow you to other websites.

Settings in your browser

The editor keeps your export settings (the format and the scale) in the local storage of your browser, so that they stay after a reload. This data does not leave your device. You can delete it in the settings of your browser.

Links to other websites

The links to GitHub and X open other websites. Their own privacy notices apply there. The editor loads nothing from these websites before you click a link.

Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to:

  • get information about the data that we process about you (Art. 15),
  • correct wrong data (Art. 16),
  • delete data (Art. 17) or limit its processing (Art. 18),
  • get your data in a common format (Art. 20),
  • object to processing that is based on our legitimate interest (Art. 21),
  • complain to a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77).

We do not store your IP address or other data about you ourselves. Only Cloudflare has the request data, for a short time.
